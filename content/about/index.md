My children and I have been making up stories about Panda Pu and her friends for over ten years.
After all that time, we thought some of them might be worth sharing. So here we go.

Panda Pu is a gentle and curious little panda. She always explores the world around her,
tries new things, and looks after her friends.

The stories have a few quirky jokes and a bit of teasing, just enough to make children laugh.

We sincerely hope you enjoy Panda Pu's adventures.
