Panda Pu is a series of gentle stories about curiosity, friendship, learning,
and discovering the world.

The books are written for children of around four to seven, to be read aloud
together or enjoyed as they grow into reading on their own. They are quiet,
warm stories, made for bedtimes, rainy afternoons and slow mornings.
